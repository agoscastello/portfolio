Context

A real estate developer without a home online

Hauston is a Buenos Aires-based real estate developer specialising in premium residential projects across sought-after neighbourhoods including Nuñez, Palermo, Recoleta, and Arcos. They work alongside prestigious architecture studios to deliver spaces that are aesthetic, versatile, and modern.

The brief was to build a complete digital presence that communicated their portfolio of projects, positioned them as a premium developer, and gave potential buyers and investors a clear path to get in touch.

My role

UX, visual design, and WordPress build

I led the project from concept through to live deployment, covering the full stack of a web project: information architecture and sitemap, visual design system, and WordPress implementation using Elementor for the page builder layer.

  • Site architecture: defining the content hierarchy across Inicio, Nosotros, Proyectos, Prensa, and Contacto
  • Individual project pages for 7 developments, each with gallery, description, and contact CTA
  • Visual system consistent with Hauston's premium positioning: dark backgrounds, high-quality photography, clean sans-serif typography
  • WordPress build with Elementor, ensuring the client could manage content updates without developer involvement
  • WhatsApp integration for direct client communication, keeping the contact path as short as possible

Design decisions

Decisions that serve a premium audience

Real estate buyers and investors scan for trust signals before they read a word of copy. Every design decision was made with that scanning behaviour in mind.

Photography first

Let the projects speak

Full-bleed hero photography on every project page. The imagery does the work of communicating quality before any copy is read.

Navigation

Projects accessible in two clicks from anywhere

The Proyectos dropdown exposes all 7 developments directly from the nav, so returning users can jump straight to a specific project.

Contact

Multiple paths to the same goal

WhatsApp float button, dedicated Contacto page, and inline CTAs on every project page. The contact intent is never more than one tap away.

Outcome

A live product the client manages independently

The site launched at hauston.com.ar, where it currently displays the full portfolio of developments across Buenos Aires. The WordPress and Elementor stack gives the client full control over content, images, and project details without requiring ongoing developer support.

Hauston now has a digital presence that matches the premium quality of their physical projects.
