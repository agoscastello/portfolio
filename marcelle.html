Context

A career coach who needed a digital home

Marcelle Kaswalder is a career coach who helps people navigate professional transitions, identify their strengths, and build careers aligned with what they actually want. She had the expertise and the clients, but no digital presence to match.

The project brief was to design a web responsive site from scratch, applying the five elements of user experience as a structured design process: strategy, scope, structure, skeleton, and surface.

Process

Five elements, one coherent product

Rather than jumping straight to wireframes, the project was structured around James Garrett's five-element model, ensuring decisions at each layer were grounded in the layer below.

Strategy

What do we want? What do users want?

Research phase to understand Marcelle's goals and what her potential clients were looking for when deciding to work with a coach.

Scope

What are we building, and what are we not?

Defining functional and content requirements, including what to prioritise in the MVP and what to defer to a later phase.

Structure

How is information organised?

Designing the navigation system, user flow, and content hierarchy based on the information architecture findings.

Skeleton

Where does everything go on screen?

Low and mid-fidelity wireframes distributing functions and information across the layout.

Surface

What does the final experience look like?

Visual design layer: components, color system, typography, and final UI applied across all screens.

Research

Two archetypes, one decision

User research revealed two distinct profiles visiting a career coach's site. Rather than designing for an abstract user, the archetypes grounded every layout and content decision.

Martina

17 years old

A high-school student finishing her last year, energetic and practical. She wants to socialise, play hockey, and finish strong academically, but she's anxious about leaving her province to study in Buenos Aires and unsure what she wants to study.

Goals

Graduate with top marks and win the hockey tournament. Maximise her remaining time in her home province.

Frustrations

Moving to a big city next year, and feeling uncertain about what direction to take professionally.

Carolina

25 years old

An organised, self-confident professional who is always upskilling. She studied law because she thought she wanted to be a lawyer, but near the end of her degree realised it wasn't her real passion.

Goals

Stay up to date and keep learning. She loves seeing how others solve similar problems, even if she ends up finding her own path.

Frustrations

Years into a career she no longer finds meaningful, and looking for guidance on how to make a change.

Information Architecture

What users actually needed on the site

IA research helped prioritise the site's sections. Three findings shaped the navigation and content hierarchy directly.

100%

Contact page

Every user surveyed needed a clear way to get in touch. The contact path became the primary CTA across all pages.

60%

Work modality

More than half needed to understand how coaching sessions work before deciding to reach out.

50%

Online platform

Half of users expected remote session options, making a clear online/in-person distinction essential.

Visual Design

Color and type that signal confidence

The color system was built around a bold violet and a warm orange, grounded in white and dark navy. The pairing positions Marcelle as modern and professional while staying approachable.

Typography was set in Roboto across all weights, using Roboto Bold at 48px for H1 display, Bold 36px for subheadings, and Regular 21px for body. The choice keeps the reading experience clean at all screen sizes.
