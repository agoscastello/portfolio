Transforming a complex, data-dense editorial platform and satellite microsites into an intuitive research tool for tax, accounting, and legal professionals.

Context

A foundational resource for legal and tax professionals

Errepar is one of the leading legal, tax, and accounting publishers in Latin America. Thousands of accountants, lawyers, and corporate advisors rely on its digital platform and specialized microsites daily to consult regulations, court rulings, and complex tax legislation where accuracy and speed are vital.

The legacy ecosystem had grown organically over decades, resulting in fragmented satellite microsites, inconsistent navigation patterns, and a search engine that failed to deliver relevant results efficiently.

Problem

When users blame themselves for bad UX

During qualitative research sessions and user interviews, a striking pattern emerged: professionals were consistently taking the blame for platform failures. Users repeatedly expressed frustration with comments like "I must be searching wrong" or "I am not good at finding things here."

In reality, the system was failing them. The interface suffered from extreme visual noise, broken content hierarchy, and a search architecture that required exact keywords rather than supporting natural professional queries. In a professional B2B setting, bad UX creates anxiety, slows down billable work, and damages user confidence.

Core challenge

How do you modernize a massive, content-dense legal repository without breaking familiar workflows for long-time power users?

My role

End to end research, strategy, and interface redesign

As UX/UI Lead, I directed the research and redesign strategy across the core platform and its specialized microsites:

  • Conducting qualitative user interviews and usability testing sessions with accountants and legal experts
  • Uncovering core usability friction points, mental model mismatches, and search failures
  • Re-architecting the global taxonomy, navigation models, and microsite cross-linking strategy
  • Redesigning search experience with contextual filters, clear validation states, and scannable result cards
  • Establishing a clean, accessible UI hierarchy optimized for high data density and prolonged reading
  • Aligning design patterns across satellite microsites to build a unified brand ecosystem

Key design decisions

Decisions tailored for high-intent researchers

Decision 01

Reframing search from keyword matching to contextual discovery

Since the search engine was the primary entry point for research, we redesigned the search interaction model. We introduced contextual auto-suggestions, clear category filters, and structured result cards that highlighted relevant legal dates and status, allowing users to verify relevance instantly without opening each document.

Decision 02

Unifying satellite microsites under a shared design system

The ecosystem contained multiple independent microsites with divergent layouts. We created a modular component library that brought visual and navigational consistency to all satellite sites, reducing learning curves and allowing users to navigate seamlessly across different tax modules without losing orientation.

Decision 03

Designing for extended reading and cognitive relief

Tax and legal consultation requires reading dense articles for hours. We redesigned the typography hierarchy, introduced high contrast reading modes conforming to WCAG 2.2 standards, and added collapsible content sidebars to help users navigate long legal texts effortlessly without losing their place in the document.

Final solution

Empowering professionals with clarity and speed

The redesigned Errepar platform and microsite network transformed a chaotic digital archive into a modern research workstation. By fixing the underlying information architecture and search UX, we shifted the user experience from self-doubt and hesitation to speed, precision, and trust.

Outcome

Restored confidence and seamless navigation

The new architecture significantly improved task completion speed for complex legal queries. User feedback post-launch showed a complete turnaround in sentiment: professionals felt empowered, navigated between microsites without losing context, and trusted the search engine to deliver accurate results on the first attempt.

Reflection

What this reinforced

This project highlighted a critical principle in B2B product design: when users blame themselves for interface struggles, it is a clear sign that the product's information architecture is broken. Redesigning Errepar was not about making a legal site look modern, but about respecting the user's expertise and building a tool that works as efficiently as they do.
